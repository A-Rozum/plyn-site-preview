# plyn-site-preview

Landing page for Plyń, one page per language at plyn.one/<code>/ (en, be, be-latn for Belarusian in Łacinka, pl, it, lt). index.html is the template; i18n.json holds the texts of every language; tools/build.py builds dist/<code>/index.html with the texts, lang, canonical and hreflang links (x-default plyn.one/). The Worker sends plyn.one/ to the visitor's chosen language (cookie plyn-lang set by the switcher, or ?lang=), else the first supported browser language, else English. css/ (core.css copied from A-Rozum/prastora at fa54c1e, themes/plyn.css with the brand colours, pages/landing.css; the build composes them into /css/site.css with Prastora's tools/styles.py, copied here, following prastora methods/css-delivery.md; no print stylesheet for this page), og.png and apple-touch-icon.png (rendered from plyn-brand by tools/images.py when the brand changes), sitemap.xml and robots.txt (built), js/site.js (behaviour), favicon.svg, review/<code>.txt for translation review (generated from i18n.json by tools/review.py).

Between the rings runs the owner's Quenya inscription (Latin text, Tengwar text and rendering, how to remake it: inscription/README.md) „Min Sirië, an hirë te, Min Sirië, an hosta te illi, Min Sirië, an lerya te illi, Ar tulya te morniello calanna.“ in Tengwar, the same in every language: transcribed with Glaemscribe (mode quenya, classical), set in Alcarin Tengwar (OFL) subset to its signs in fonts/alcarin-ring.woff2 (pyftsubset --text-file, --layout-features=*); written as on the One Ring: no spaces between words, a low pusta and a space at the end of each poetic line (Min Sirië an hirë te · Min Sirië an hosta te illi · Min Sirië an lerya te illi · Ar tulya te morniello calanna), a quadruple pusta where the end meets the start. It kindles and fades while the rings turn.

Everything that appears on the page develops (проявляется) – from transparent and soft to sharp, over about a second – and nothing appears instantly: the ring inscription, the launch lines, the dialog, the form notice, the line of the silver bullet. New appearing elements use the develop animation in css/pages/landing.css.

Crumbs for the attentive. The owner scatters hidden hints of where Plyń is heading – findable by a careful visitor, never turned into slogans or headlines; most will go unnoticed, and that is accepted. Keep them when changing the page, keep them findable and quiet, and add new ones to this list:
- the ring inscription above, an inverted One Ring verse;
- the theme switch is a yin-yang (it turns half round on hover): light and dark, and the balance Plyń keeps between them;
- the silver bullet: the silver primer – a ring with a disc inside, drawn like © – in place of the separator between Privacy and Built with in the footer; on hover or tap its line develops above it: „Silver bullets aren’t found. They’re cast.“ (an answer to Brooks’ No Silver Bullet: a silver bullet cast from accumulated institutions – methods, specifications, formal languages – towards which Plyń has taken steps); the same line is written to the browser console. It stays in English on every language page.

The mark, the wordmark and the favicon are copies of files in A-Rozum/plyn-brand (the only source). The page inlines them so the creature can blink and wave and the line can follow the theme.

Changes go on an agent/codex/ branch, land with `python .system/tools/plyn_land.py` (GH_TOKEN set) and are deployed from main afterwards.

## Hosting: plyn.one on Cloudflare

Decided by the owner (Andrey Rozum): Cloudflare hosts the page and handles the beta form; plyn.one stays registered at Porkbun with Cloudflare nameservers. The Cloudflare API token and Porkbun API keys are in the owner's preferences; mask them in any output.

- Deploy: `CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN=… GH_TOKEN=… python tools/deploy.py` from this environment (GitHub Actions minutes are out until about 1 November). It sets the house dash in i18n.json, index.html, js/site.js and the Worker (spaced en dash, `tools/typography.py`), syncs the drawings from plyn-brand (`tools/sync_brand.py`, then `--check`), rebuilds review/ from I18N (`tools/review.py`, then `--check`), builds and uploads dist/ as static assets and worker/worker.js as Worker `plyn-site` on plyn.one and www.plyn.one (www redirects to the apex). IDs of the account, zone and database are at the top of the script.
- Beta form: POST /api/beta to the same Worker. Stored in D1 `plyn-beta` (EU jurisdiction, schema in worker/schema.sql): email (key), note, language, time. Silent protection: invisible Turnstile (sitekey in index.html, secret only as the Worker secret TURNSTILE_SECRET; a deploy keeps it), hidden honeypot field `website`, 5 requests per minute per IP. Each sign-up is mailed from noreply@beta.plyn.one to plyn.one@proton.me through Email Routing on the subdomain beta.plyn.one (beta-related mail comes from it; the future beta can live there too).
- Read sign-ups: D1 query API, `SELECT * FROM signups ORDER BY created_at`.
- Statistics: D1 `plyn-stats` (EU), table hits: page views of the language pages (kind view, recorded by the Worker) and, from the page via POST /api/hit, form opened (open), form sent (submit) and leaving (leave: ms on the page, interacted) – with language, referring host, utm, country, device type and a bot flag; no cookies, no IP. The data notice says so in every language. Summary: `python tools/stats.py [days]`, with search phrases from Bing and Google (item 2 below). Search phrases come from Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools once plyn.one is verified there (a TXT record in the Cloudflare zone). Unique and returning visitors and A/B tests need a stored visitor id, hence a consent bar; the owner wants it unobtrusive, in place of the dev plate once that is removed.
- Turnstile loads only when the form opens; the launch line under the button is five texts (line1–line5) that CSS rotates; tools/build.py writes css/ticker.css with each line's time from its length plus a fixed jitter (5–10 s), per language.
